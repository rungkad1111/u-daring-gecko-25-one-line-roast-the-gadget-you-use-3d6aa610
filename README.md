A one-line roast of the smartwatch: it counts your steps, grades your sleep
and nags you hourly to stand — then dies by dinner and closes its rings
sitting in the charger.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) writes the single line to
`/output/entry.txt`, within the competition's 1–200 character gate.
